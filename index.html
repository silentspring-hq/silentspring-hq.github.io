Welcome to Silent Spring HQ



This website is the Silent Spring creative archive, which contains music, poetry, art, and photography that we want to share with the world. We will also include links and shoutouts to other artists that we admire and hopefully work towards making our internet a little bit more creative, friendly, and communal. Please enjoy the material you find here, be kind to others, and feel free to get in touch and to share your thoughts :)


What is Silent Spring

Silent Spring is a musical entity named after the seminal work of Rachel Carson. Our goal is to make incisive music that prioritises human creativity, experimentation, and intensity. We also believe in the freedom of art to exist and be shared through our own channels, which is why this website exists. In making art, and communicating it to you, we hope to partake in the revolutionary process of providing and inspiring an alternative to the alogrithms, trends, and corporate influence that has so far blighted creative spaces, independent and industrial alike. This website exists to communicate our band, our art, and our message, in its purest form, without concern as to how third party software assesses it and determines its quality for you. We hope that you find this message in any way useful and that it can inspire you to try your own hand in sharing your creativity with the world. If you feel so inclined, we would be happy to provide assistance and share resources, just drop us an email and we'll be happy to support you.

Silent Spring reserves the rights to all of our original material and work. We encourage people to play, remix, and otherwise interact with what we share, however we will seek to remove iterative work if it is used in a harmful or otherwise malicious way. (C) 2026
